# Project Goal

Create a handcrafted intarsia wall piece as a gift for the Dolphin Family Restaurant.

The piece should:

- express warmth, hospitality and family
- reflect the restaurant's welcoming atmosphere
- incorporate the restaurant's two-dolphin identity
- showcase fine intarsia craftsmanship
- avoid looking like a reproduction of their logo

This is intended as an artistic interpretation rather than advertising.

# Construction Constraints

## General

- Traditional intarsia
- Organic outer boundary
- No surrounding frame
- Structurally self-supporting
- Avoid fragile appendages
- Buildable with normal intarsia techniques

# Intarsia Principles

## Piece boundaries should perform multiple jobs.

Whenever possible a joint should simultaneously:

- improve composition
- improve glue-up
- strengthen structure
- suggest motion
- improve grain direction

---

## Water

Water is NOT simply background.

Water should:

- provide structure
- connect the composition
- imply movement
- provide graceful piece boundaries
- remain visually secondary

Water should NOT become a giant brown panel.

Instead it should consist of several flowing pieces whose joints imply motion.

---

## Large Pieces

Advantages

- fewer pieces
- easier glue-up
- attractive uninterrupted grain

Disadvantages

- fewer opportunities for micro-adjustment during assembly
- error accumulates over long distances
- reduced ability to suggest motion with joints

Large pieces are not automatically better.

---

## Structural Integrity

Avoid:

- tiny necks
- reeds
- flowers connected by narrow stems
- decorative elements existing solely for appearance

Each major element should contribute to overall strength.
